Key findings established by hand-tracing the assembly (not the
decompiler alone):

- Gap-copy guard is `SUB; JLE` (signed ≤ 0 skips): copy iff
  `(off - cur) > 0` signed. Head (`0x4056a1`), tail (`0x4057a8`)
  identical shape.
- Copy lanes: `EAX = cur+param_2`, `EDX = cur+param_1`,
  `ECX = len` — i.e. `02fbc(p2+cur, p1+cur, n)` at both
  `0x4056ab` and `0x4057b1`.
- Entry load is a double deref at both levels: `EDX=[EDI]`,
  `EDX=[EDX]` (`0x4056b3/0x4056b5`).
- `0x0d` arm pushes `[u+pc+2]` then `[u+pc+6]`; callee `0x4057bc`
  ends `RET 0x4` (pops one), so the caller `POP EAX` retrieves
  `[u+pc+2]` as the step while `[u+pc+6]` was the callee count.
- `0x0e` arm recurses into self `0x40567c` with `(p1+i3,
  p2+i3, pc)`; step `*(pc+u+2)` popped after.
- Fail tail (`0x4056ed`): EAX still holds `i3` (loaded at
  `0x4056b0`, untouched by the `CMP CL` cascade), so `MOV AL,2`
  yields `(i3 & ~0xff) | 2`.
- Early exit pops 4 regs but not the `PUSH ECX` limit slot, so
  the leftover dword is the tail's `[ESP]` lane; see Unknowns.
- `0x0c` arm passes `EDX = i3+param_2` in-register to `0x405664`,
  which takes one param and ignores it.
