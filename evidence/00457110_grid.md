## 1. Claim

`FUN_00457110` is a per-frame tick (thiscall, `ECX` = object in `ESI`,
one `float param_1` = dt): it rate-limits slot `+0x1b4` toward an indexed
target, runs a 10-way switch on global `DAT_0061b8a8` (`0-9`, default =
skip) that stores takeoff-marker triples (`00538660` out) into
`+0x190/+0x19c/+0x1a8`, then a common tail gates two strip-xform
alternatives behind the b58/b68 predicates and max-clamps the result
into `+0x1a0`, and finally emits the three triples via `00538680` into
`00451c20`/`0040cca0`. It places no frames itself; the switch bodies are
engine plumbing (physics/scene setup), not transcribed. Two pure float
fragments (head approach-step, tail max-clamp) are transcribed to
`engine/src/fn_00457110.rs` with one test.
