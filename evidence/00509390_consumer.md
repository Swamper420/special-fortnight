## 1. Claim

- `FUN_00509390` is the **rate setter**: stores the u32 rate arg into
  `[ECX+0x3c]`, then — iff guard byte `[[ECX+0x20]+0x28]` (`0040e500`) is
  nonzero — forwards it to vtable slot `+0x44` of the inner object at
  `[ECX+0x24]`. Unset init byte `[ECX+0x45]`, or a negative vtable return,
  throws `CMExceptionDSBuffer` via `005090b0` + `__CxxThrowException`.
- `FUN_005092c0` is the **level setter**: stores the f32 level arg into
  `[ECX+0x34]`, then — under the same `0040e500` gate — forwards an
  attenuation int to vtable slot `+0x3c` of `[ECX+0x24]`:
  `p = level * [ECX+0x40]` (x87); `!(p > 0.0)` (covers `<= 0` and NaN) →
  `-10000`; else `__ftol(1000.0 * log2(p))` clamped to `[-10000, 0]`
  (underflow floors at `-10000`, positive truncations zeroed).
- `FUN_00509460` is the **gain setter**: `[ECX+0x40] = gain`, then
  re-applies the stored level via `005092c0([ECX+0x34])` — confirms `+0x40`
  is the gain multiplier and `+0x34` the stored level.
- Together with `005090b0`'s `"Direct Sound Buffer failure"` /
  `CMExceptionDSBuffer`, both are methods on a DirectSound-buffer wrapper
  class (inference — vtable targets are indirect, no API claimed).

## 6. Inference

- Role: DirectSound-buffer wrapper setters. Rate passes through untouched
  (clamp is caller-side); level maps linear→attenuation
  (`1000·log₂(level·gain)`, silence floor `-10000`, full-scale `0`),
  consistent with a volume-in-hundredths-of-dB convention — but the
  vtable slots (`+0x3c`/`+0x44`) are indirect with unmapped targets, so no
  API-level claim (e.g. `SetVolume`/`SetFrequency`) is made.
- `+0x44`(level) vs `+0x45`(rate) init bytes are distinct per setter;
  object-creation / flag-set sites unmapped.

## 7. Transcription decision: evidence only (no code)

- `00509390` has no pure fragment: init-guard + store + liveness-guard +
  indirect forward are all object/vtable plumbing with an unmapped target.
- `005092c0`'s attenuation (`1000·log₂`, `[-10000,0]` clamp) looks pure
  but is NOT clean: the log runs in x87 80-bit (`FYL2X`/`FDIVP` change of
  base) where `f64::log2` rounding can differ ±1 ulp at int boundaries,
  and `__ftol` yields `INT_MIN` on non-finite input (→ clamped `-10000`)
  while Rust `as i32` saturates toward `MAX` (→ clamped `0`) — a real
  divergence needing speculative branches. Per evidence-first rules the
  guess stays out of `engine/src`.
- 1:1 reuse check: `engine/src/fn_004586a0.rs` already holds the
  caller-side `rate_clamp` / `clamp_lo_hi`; nothing here duplicates them.
