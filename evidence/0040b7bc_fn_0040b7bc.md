  true-exit epilogue at `0x0040b844` provably sets
  `EAX=EBX` (skip-ws cursor), and all three callers

  the next parse input). Rust returns `*mut u16`.
- Inference: thunks `0x004054ac`/`0x00404c88` are plain `JMP`
